\noindent\textbf{Figure R1. Cosine similarity: \texttt{cos\_sim\_exp\_tens} / \texttt{cosSimExpTens}.}
Rules are tested top to bottom; the first that fires decides. Dashed edges are \texttt{method} overrides. Both languages share every rule; only fitted constants differ.
\vspace{2mm}

\begin{forest} routing
[{\textbf{cosine}(x, y, method)\\input forms: density pair; density lists (per pair);\\raw arrays (build, then pair); batched single-multiset rows (per row)}
  [{empty operand} [{\textit{any side has no events}\\$0$}, leaf]]
  [{\textit{yes}\\ordered attribute ([sym]=0, $r>1$)\\on either side}
    [{\textit{overrides 'mobius'/'centres' silently}\\Bulger enumeration\\(perm $\times$ comb, $r!$)}, leaf]]
  [{nested attribute present\\and method $\neq$ 'bulger'}
    [{\textit{auto / contract / mobius / centres}\\\textbf{nested plan} (Figure R3)\\declines $\to$ Bulger under 'auto',\\error under a forced method}]]
  [{method $\neq$ 'auto' (flat)}
    [{Bulger}, leaf, ovr={bulger}]
    [{unrestricted centres\\(perm $\times$ perm)}, leaf, ovr={centres}]
    [{M\"obius per-attribute\\(Figure R2)}, leaf, ovr={mobius}]

  [{\textbf{flat selector} ('auto'), rules in order}
    [{1. $r_{\max}\le 1$} [{Bulger}, leaf]]
    [{2. $r_{\max}>8$} [{Bulger; feasibility guard\\$n_J^x n_J^y\cdot 8 > $ budget $\to$ error}, leaf]]
    [{3. working set $n_J\,2\Sigma r\cdot 8 > 256$\,MB\\(and not rel-per above threshold)} [{M\"obius}, leaf]]
    [{4. rel-per and $\sigma/P > \mathrm{thr}(t_s)$}
      [{\textit{wrap='single-image'}\\Bulger (minimum-image measure)}, leaf]
      [{\textit{wrap='full-image'}\\M\"obius (all-image measure)}, leaf]
      [{mixed wrap on one density}, err]]
    [{5. cost race\\$c_\mathrm{B}=$ law(bulger, tuple-pair entries)\\$c_\mathrm{M}=\sum_a \max(\min(\mathrm{centres},\mathrm{grid}),\ \mathrm{floor})$\\(memoised self products skipped on both sides)}
      [{\textit{$c_\mathrm{B}\le c_\mathrm{M}$}\\Bulger}, leaf]
      [{\textit{$c_\mathrm{B}> c_\mathrm{M}$}\\M\"obius (Figure R2)\\post-hoc guard: impossible value $\to$ Bulger}, leaf]]]

\vspace{4mm}
\noindent\textbf{Kernel core shared by the Bulger and centres arms} (per attribute, log kernel over tuple pairs):
Gram form when non-periodic and accurate enough; inner-[rel] block form when $r_\mathrm{in}>0$;
abs-per full-image $\theta$ (wrapped Gaussian, image sum or Fourier) when wrap='full-image' and $L>0$;
otherwise nearest-image reduction and the quadratic form $Q$ (rel-per: pairwise minimum-image wrap). All-$r=1$ densities take the direct kernel sum.

\noindent\textbf{Figure R2. The M\"obius arm: one $(N_x\times N_y)$ matrix per attribute.}
\vspace{2mm}

\begin{forest} routing
[{\textbf{per attribute} $a$}
  [{$r_a = 1$} [{direct kernel sum (abs kernel;\\wrap honoured)}, leaf]]
  [{absolute, $r_a\ge 2$}
    [{\textit{non-per, $K_xK_y\ge 2\cdot10^5$, nnz $\le 0.2K_xK_y$}\\sparse orbit sub-matrix}, leaf]
    [{\textit{else}\\dense orbit (pw-batched)}, leaf]]
  [{relative, $r_a\ge 2$:\\centres-vs-grid gate\\(\texttt{\_ma\_rel\_attr\_prefers\_centres})}
    [{\textit{admissible and $c_\mathrm{centres}<c_\mathrm{grid}$ (or rel\_attr\_route='centres')}\\tuple-centres closed form\\comb-side restriction; $L=0$ short-circuit}, leaf]
    [{translation grid\\$N_u$ nodes; image sum on periodic}
      [{\textit{$2\le r\le4$, grid $\le 4\cdot10^6$, grid $\le 1100\,K_x^2\,N_xN_y$}\\spectral (Fourier) contraction}, leaf]
      [{\textit{else}\\lattice contraction\\(orbit grid / pw-batched)}, leaf]]
    [{'centres' requested above $\sigma/P$ threshold\\on a full-image attribute}, err]]
]
\end{forest}

\vspace{4mm}
The centres route is blocked by measure when rel-per and $\sigma/P>\mathrm{thr}(t_s)$ (it computes the minimum-image reading), and by cost otherwise; a forced 'mobius' keeps the grid. Both routes memoise the self products under the key \texttt{mobius|}$t_s$\texttt{|choices}.

      [{\textit{yes}\\M\"obius}, leaf] [{\textit{no}\\centres}, leaf]]]
  [{\textbf{centres branch}}
    [{single multiset: kernel sum\\1-D vectorised | bucketed | circular | exact chunks\\chunk: $\theta$ product ($L>0$) | tabulated $\theta$ | nearest-image $Q$}, leaf]
    [{MA, all $r\ge2$, no kernel covariance: factored\\(per-attribute kernel sums)}, leaf]
    [{MA otherwise: joint tuple centres\\(tabulated $\theta$ when values repeat)}, leaf]]
  [{\textbf{M\"obius branch}}
    [{absolute: partition sum, reduction rule}, leaf]
    [{relative: $u$-grid; direct | factored (non-per) | Fourier ($2\le r\le4$)}, leaf]]

\end{forest}

\vspace{4mm}
\noindent\textbf{Entropy} (\texttt{entropy\_exp\_tens}): 'differential' $\to$ adaptive grid of point evaluations (eval 'auto'); 'shannon' / 'normalized' $\to$ erf cell masses on absolute densities (minimum-image on periodic axes), grid via eval otherwise; 'renyi2' $\to$ $\langle T,T\rangle$ from the per-attribute M\"obius matrices (Figure R2), numerical for nested or ordered attributes.
\textbf{Windowed similarity}: every window position is a cosine call under 'auto'; \texttt{windowed\_tensor\_similarity} has a single closed-form path.
\textbf{Sweep}: mixture (dedup pairs) vs orbit sweep by pair count and chunk budget; the orbit sweep reuses Figure R2's per-attribute matrices.
